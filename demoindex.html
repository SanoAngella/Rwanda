About the transformation

From rebuilding to designing a regional model for progress.

Known as the Land of a Thousand Hills, Rwanda has become one of Africa's clearest examples of long-term national rebuilding. The country's recent development is visible in infrastructure, energy access, digital ambition, and sustainable urban growth.

This page gathers major projects into one clean visual story. Each project is presented as part of a bigger system: mobility, power, technology, environment, and daily life.
