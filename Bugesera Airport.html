Bugesera International Airport

A new aviation gateway designed to expand Rwanda's passenger, cargo, tourism, and business connections.

Bugesera District Airport hub Green design

Located about 25 km south of Kigali, Bugesera International Airport is planned near Rilima in Rwanda's Eastern Province.

Back to projects

Overview

A larger front door for Rwanda.

The airport is one of Rwanda's most ambitious infrastructure projects. It is planned as a modern passenger and cargo hub that supports tourism, trade, and RwandAir's international growth.

Project details

  • Phase oneCapacity for about 1.7 million passengers, a 4.2 km runway, and modern terminal facilities.
  • Future expansionLong-term growth toward several million passengers each year with added airport infrastructure.
  • InvestmentThe project is backed by the Government of Rwanda and Qatar Airways.
  • ContractorMota-Engil has been involved in major construction works.

Design direction

  • EnergySolar power systems and efficient lighting reduce operating impact.
  • WaterRainwater harvesting and recycling support smarter resource use.
  • TravelSmart check-in, passenger lounges, cargo areas, and duty-free spaces improve the airport experience.
  • ImpactThe project is expected to create jobs, attract investors, and connect Rwanda more closely to the world.
