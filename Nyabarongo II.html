05 / Power

Nyabarongo II Dam

A clean-energy project helping Rwanda expand reliable electricity and manage water resources.

Nyabarongo River 43.5 MW Hydropower

The dam is located on the Nyabarongo River near Ruhango and Muhanga, supporting electricity generation and water management.

Back to projects

Overview

Reliable electricity from Rwanda's river system.

Nyabarongo II is part of Rwanda's renewable-energy strategy, complementing other hydropower projects and strengthening national energy security.

Project details

  • TypeHydroelectric project designed around river flow and dam infrastructure.
  • CapacityPlanned output of about 43.5 MW.
  • PurposeGenerate electricity, regulate flow, and support irrigation.
  • FundingAdvanced by Rwanda with support from development partners.

Impact

  • EnergyExpands clean electricity for households and businesses.
  • EnvironmentReduces pressure on fossil-fuel generation.
  • CommunityCreates local jobs during construction and operation.
  • FutureSupports Vision 2050 through renewable infrastructure.
