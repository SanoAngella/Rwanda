04 / Energy

Rusumo Hydroelectric Station

A regional renewable-energy project shared by Rwanda, Tanzania, and Burundi.

Kagera River 80 MW Regional cooperation

The station is located at Rusumo Falls on the Kagera River, near the border area shared by Rwanda, Tanzania, and Burundi.

Back to projects

Overview

Shared clean power for East Africa.

The Rusumo Hydroelectric Power Project supplies affordable renewable electricity and stands as a practical example of regional planning, shared infrastructure, and green development.

Project details

  • TypeRun-of-river hydroelectric power plant.
  • CapacityInstalled capacity of 80 MW shared among the three partner countries.
  • FundingSupported by institutions including the World Bank and African Development Bank.
  • ImplementationAdvanced through regional cooperation under the Nile Basin development framework.

Impact

  • AccessImproves electricity reliability for households and industry.
  • EnvironmentReduces dependence on diesel power generation.
  • EconomyCreates jobs and supports local economic activity.
  • RegionStrengthens cooperation between Rwanda, Tanzania, and Burundi.
