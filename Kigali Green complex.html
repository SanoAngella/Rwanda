Kigali Green Complex

A modern commercial building that puts sustainability into the center of Kigali's business growth.

Downtown Kigali Commercial Sustainable building

The Kigali Green Complex sits near the central business district and represents Rwanda's push toward cleaner, smarter urban infrastructure.

Back to projects

Overview

A working building with a green public message.

Developed by the Rwanda Social Security Board, the complex combines office, retail, and service spaces while showing how a fast-growing city can build with environmental responsibility.

Design and use

  • MaterialsBuilt with sustainable design choices and lower-impact systems.
  • EnergyUses efficient lighting, ventilation, and renewable-energy features.
  • ProgramHosts offices, shops, banks, restaurants, conference spaces, and parking.
  • PurposeSupports Kigali's business district while making green construction visible.

Impact

  • EconomicCreates jobs in construction, operations, and services.
  • City imageStrengthens Kigali's reputation as a clean and modern capital.
  • WorkplaceEncourages digital, efficient, and lower-waste working environments.
  • ExampleShows other developments how sustainability can be part of commercial value.
