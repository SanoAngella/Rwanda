Kigali Innovation City

A district designed to connect education, research, startups, and digital investment in one place.

Gasabo District Technology Vision 2050

Kigali Innovation City is planned in the Kigali Special Economic Zone, where business, education, technology, and research can reinforce each other.

Back to projects

Overview

A campus for Rwanda's knowledge economy.

KIC supports Rwanda's goal of becoming a technology-led economy by bringing universities, labs, startups, offices, and lifestyle spaces into one coordinated innovation district.

Main components

  • EducationDesigned to host universities and specialist learning institutions.
  • ResearchLabs and research centers support applied innovation.
  • BusinessOffices and co-working spaces create room for startups and investors.
  • LivingResidential and lifestyle spaces make the district more complete.

Technology and partners

  • InfrastructureReliable digital systems support education and enterprise.
  • SustainabilityEnergy-efficient buildings and green systems shape the plan.
  • PartnersSupported through Rwanda's development ecosystem and international investment partners.
  • GoalGrow talent, entrepreneurship, and research capacity inside Rwanda.
